Privacy Policy — Waffle Street

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Waffle Street ("the game") is made by DestecGames. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

  • No account, no name, no email. The game does not ask for or store personal information.
  • Game progress (coins, recipes, settings, the age you choose at the first start) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Age question. At the first start the game asks your age. It is stored only on your device and is used only to choose the right type of ads (see below).

Advertising

The game can show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to get a bonus). Ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • For players under 13, ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and only non-personalized ads are shown. No advertising identifier is used for personalization.
  • For players 13 and older, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier according to your device settings and Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • All ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • Buying the "No Ads" pack removes all ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The game is designed to be suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
